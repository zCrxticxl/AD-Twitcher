## English (en)

**Description**

```
AD-Twitcher claims your Twitch drops and channel points automatically.

- Claims completed drops from your Twitch Drops inventory, reloading the page first when its view is out of date
- Claims channel point bonus chests as they appear
- Mutes ad breaks on the browser tab, so playback and watch time keep counting
- Optionally covers the player while an ad is running
- Resumes playback when the player pauses on its own
- Opens followed channels when they go live
- Shows viewer and chat activity for the channel you are watching
- Keeps a running count of everything it claimed for you
- Available in 12 languages

Everything stays in your browser. No account, no analytics, no external services.

AD-Twitcher is not an ad blocker: Twitch ads keep playing while the tab is muted. Drop progress is controlled by Twitch, and the extension only claims drops that Twitch has already marked as completed.

Not affiliated with Twitch Interactive, Inc.
```

**Changelog**

```
1.0.6
- Drops are claimed without a manual page reload. An out-of-date inventory tab is refreshed first.
- Ad breaks are muted on the browser tab instead of the Twitch player, so no watch time is lost.
- Playback resumes when the player pauses on its own. A pause you started is left alone.
- The installed version is shown in the popup footer.
```
