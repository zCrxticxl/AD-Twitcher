# AD-Twitcher

## Short description

Claim Twitch bonuses and drops, mute ad breaks, follow live channels and view local activity metrics.

## Detailed description

AD-Twitcher keeps repetitive Twitch tasks in one focused browser extension.

Features:

- Claims available channel point bonus chests
- Claims completed drops from the Twitch Drops inventory
- Warns when a stream tab stalls and Drops or channel points may stop progressing
- Protects monitored Twitch tabs from automatic discarding and attempts one recovery reload
- Mutes ad breaks and optionally covers the player while an ad is running
- Restores the player automatically when the ad ends
- Opens selected followed channels when they go live
- Displays measured viewer and chat activity without claiming to detect bots
- Keeps local lifetime counters for claimed bonuses, drops, muted ad breaks and opened streams
- Available in 12 interface languages

Your settings, counters and measurements remain in your browser. AD-Twitcher does not use analytics, remote code or external data services.

Important: AD-Twitcher is not an ad blocker. Twitch ads continue to play while the player is muted and optionally covered. Drop progress is controlled by Twitch; the extension only claims drops that Twitch already marks as completed.

AD-Twitcher is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Twitch Interactive, Inc.
