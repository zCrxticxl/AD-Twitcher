AD-Twitcher 1.0.10

What's new:
- A farming tab you close yourself is no longer reopened straight away. Farming pauses; "Apply queue" or switching auto-farming off and on resumes it. If the whole window closes, farming carries on in a new tab.
- With "Then open the inventory in a background tab" switched off, an inventory tab you already have open is still used to claim finished drops.

Fixes:
- The background inventory tab is only closed while it still shows the drops inventory, never after you navigated it elsewhere.
- Firefox before 126: the farming tab is muted again (the combined mute/keep-alive update was rejected).
- No false "drop unlocked" detection from the extension's own message in Russian and Japanese.
- Settings number fields stay within their allowed range; a failed full reset is reported.
- Content scripts are no longer injected twice on install or browser start.

No new permissions, no data collection - all data stays on your device.
