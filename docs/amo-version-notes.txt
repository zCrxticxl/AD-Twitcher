AD-Twitcher 1.0.9

What's new:
- Settings tab redesigned: grouped cards, numeric default values shown, per-card Reset buttons, a separate Danger zone for the global reset, and a confirmation toast on save.
- Personal channel statistics: opt-in local watch-time tracking per channel under Watch Health, with weekly/monthly totals and a per-channel ranking.
- A Drops includeInStats option lets farmed channels count toward your own channel statistics.
- Farming robustness: farming tab ownership survives a browser restart, the extension never takes over tabs you opened yourself, campaigns farm even with automatic claiming off, and expired campaigns leave the queue automatically.
- The drop list is now authoritative: a small or empty (successfully read) drop list removes stale campaigns instead of keeping them.

Fixes:
- After a browser restart the extension recovers its own farming tab instead of opening a duplicate and orphaning the old one.
- A settings change made while Drops discovery was in flight is now applied immediately afterwards instead of being dropped.
- An empty inventory no longer resurrects a campaign you removed or that genuinely ended.
- Disabling Watch Health now also stops personal channel history recording.

No new permissions, no data collection - all data stays on your device.
