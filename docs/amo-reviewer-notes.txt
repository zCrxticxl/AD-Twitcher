Notes for the reviewer - AD-Twitcher 1.0.10

Permissions:
- storage: settings, activity state, counters, drop progress and channel
  statistics - all local
- tabs: check for already-open channel/inventory tabs, manage the farming tab
- alarms: periodic checks
- notifications: "stream may be stalled" watchdog and farming notifications
- *://*.twitch.tv/*: all functionality runs on Twitch pages only
- Firefox uses a persistent background page.

Data handling:
- No telemetry, analytics, remote servers or background network requests.
- Stored settings and activity stay in browser.storage.local; short diagnostic
  logs stay in memory. Nothing is transmitted by the extension.

How to verify the 1.0.10 changes:
1. Farming pause: enable auto-farming, let it open its tab, then close that
   tab by hand. No new tab opens and the popup shows the paused state;
   "Apply queue" resumes farming.
2. Open inventory: switch off "Then open the inventory in a background tab",
   open twitch.tv/drops/inventory yourself and press the drops check in the
   popup. Claims run in that tab and no extra tab opens.
3. Inventory tab closer: when the extension opens the inventory in a
   background tab, navigate that tab to a channel before it closes. It stays
   open.
4. Farming tab audio: the farming tab is muted (relevant for Firefox < 126).

Source:
- Unminified, in the src/ folder; node build.mjs --zip reproduces the
  packaged zip.
